Services · Wealth Management

Strategic Wealth Management for Complex Financial Landscapes

At UHY CAPITAL, Shtainmetz Aminoach, we provide tailored wealth management solutions for high-net-worth individuals, families, and corporate stakeholders. Our approach combines investment strategy, financial structuring, and long-term planning within a coordinated global framework designed to preserve, grow, and transfer wealth across generations.

01

Investment Strategy & Advanced Financial Solutions

Access to institutional-quality opportunities, selected according to each client's objectives, risk profile and wealth structure.

Investment Funds

We provide access to a wide range of investment funds in Israel and internationally, tailoring investment strategies to each client's objectives, risk profile, and wealth structure. Our approach emphasizes diversification, risk management, and long-term financial perspective.

Through relationships with leading financial institutions, we provide access to advanced investment opportunities across private and institutional markets.

Hedge Funds

We provide access to hedge funds and alternative investment solutions designed for sophisticated and accredited investors. These solutions offer strategic diversification and exposure to advanced risk-return management models.

Investment structures are selected according to the client's objectives, market environment, and long-term wealth preservation strategy.

Private Family Funds

We advise wealthy families on the planning and establishment of private investment structures designed to centralize, manage, and preserve family wealth across generations.

These structures provide flexibility, governance, and alignment between family objectives, investment strategy, and long-term continuity planning.

Structured Equity Products

We provide access to structured equity solutions tailored to investors seeking customized exposure, diversification, and advanced risk management strategies.

These solutions enable flexible investment approaches beyond traditional market instruments while aligning with the client's broader wealth objectives.

02

Wealth Structuring, Protection & Continuity

Structures that preserve assets, maintain control and carry family wealth in an orderly way across generations.

Financial Asset Protection & Structuring

We develop sophisticated financial protection structures designed to preserve assets, mitigate exposure, and maintain financial control within complex domestic and international environments.

Our approach integrates strategic planning, legal structuring, and regulatory considerations as part of a comprehensive wealth management framework.

Family Asset Management

We support families in the coordination and management of financial assets, ownership structures, and intergenerational planning.

The objective is to create a structured, transparent, and coordinated framework that supports long-term financial stability and continuity.

Intergenerational Planning

We assist clients in planning long-term family continuity and wealth transition strategies while preserving family interests, ownership structures, and future financial stability.

Each structure is tailored to the family's financial, business, and cross-border complexity.

Wealth Transfer, Succession & Estate Planning

We design long-term strategies for wealth transfer, succession planning, and estate structuring aimed at preserving family wealth in an efficient and controlled manner.

These solutions integrate financial, legal, and international considerations within a comprehensive wealth architecture framework.

03

Financial Planning, Insurance & Retirement Solutions

A stable protection framework that supports both personal and family continuity.

Insurance & Pension Solutions

We provide insurance and pension solutions as part of a broader financial planning strategy tailored to each client's wealth structure, exposures, and long-term objectives.

Our approach focuses on building a stable financial protection framework that supports both personal and family continuity.

Retirement Planning

We advise clients on strategic retirement planning designed to preserve lifestyle, financial stability, and long-term capital sustainability.

Planning considerations include investment structures, future financial needs, and cash flow management within the broader context of family wealth.

Special Risk Insurance Solutions (Directors & Officers Liability)

We provide specialized insurance solutions designed to protect directors, shareholders, and executives from complex liabilities and financial exposures.

These solutions are tailored according to the client's corporate structure, regulatory environment, and operational risk profile.

International Solutions & Strategic Planning

Cross-border efficiency, compliance and institutional access, coordinated within one global structure.

International Tax Planning

We provide international tax planning solutions tailored to individuals, families, and corporations operating across multiple jurisdictions.

Our approach combines tax efficiency, regulatory compliance, and long-term planning within a coordinated global financial structure.

Strategic Partnerships with Leading Institutional Entities in Israel and Abroad

Through strategic relationships with leading financial institutions in Israel and internationally, we provide clients with access to advanced investment opportunities, banking solutions, and institutional-level financial capabilities.
