The strategy

A different approach to the FX market

LOTUS ONE applies an intraday, fully-hedged trading strategy based on capturing short-term inefficiencies in the FX market.

Intraday trading

Most activity takes place within the trading day, aiming to reduce overnight exposure.

Relative value

Exploiting price gaps and short-term inefficiencies between currency pairs.

Full hedge

Opening long and short positions simultaneously, as part of the strategy's hedging mechanism.

High liquidity

Activity in the major currency pairs, in the market with the highest daily trading volume in the world.

Built on investment discipline

Principles of the strategy

Capital preservation

A contractual commitment to a 10% loss cap, supporting downside protection and capital preservation.

Full hedge

Long and short positions simultaneously as part of the strategy.

Minimal overnight exposure

Most positions are closed by the end of the trading day, to reduce overnight exposure.

Monthly liquidity

NAV is calculated on the last business day of each month.

Eligible for IRA investment

0% management fee

Selective commodity exposure
