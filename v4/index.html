About

A single, coherent architecture for substantial wealth

UHY Capital is a wealth management and Family Office platform providing a comprehensive, integrated framework for high-net-worth families, controlling shareholders and entrepreneurs who qualify as eligible clients. Its activity is built upon the professional infrastructure of Shtainmetz Aminoach & Co., one of Israel's leading firms in complex taxation and financial planning, and upon the international UHY network operating in approximately 100 countries.

We provide a holistic offering spanning international tax planning, trust structures, investment management, alternative assets, family governance and intergenerational planning, all under one consolidated view of the family's wealth.
