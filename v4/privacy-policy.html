1. Introduction and Scope of the Policy

The companies comprising the Shtainmetz Aminoach & Co., Certified Public Accountants Group (hereinafter: the "Group") attach great importance to protecting the privacy of their clients and recognize the importance of safeguarding their personal information.

The Group respects the privacy of users of this website and, for this purpose, implements information security measures as required under applicable law, and in particular pursuant to the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (hereinafter: the "Protection of Privacy Law") and the regulations promulgated thereunder.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to describe the manner in which the Group collects, uses, stores, and transfers information relating to users.

Use of the website and/or the Group's services constitutes the user's consent to this Privacy Policy. If the user does not agree to the provisions of this Privacy Policy, the user should refrain from continuing to use the website and the services.

The user acknowledges that any information provided by the user is provided voluntarily and at the user's own initiative and consent, and that the user is not legally required to provide personal information, except for mandatory fields that are required as a condition for using certain services available through the website.

The Group may collect the following types of information:

3.1. Personal Information Provided by the User

Personal information voluntarily provided by the user in connection with contacting the Group, registering for services, or using such services, including name, identification number, address, telephone number, email address, contact details, and any additional information provided by the user.

3.2. Technical and Statistical Information

Technical and statistical information collected automatically when using the website, including IP address, browser type, operating system, times and patterns of website use, approximate device location, pages and content viewed, sources from which the user arrived at the website, and other usage characteristics.

3.3. Aggregated Information

Aggregated information concerning user activity on the website, including activity by users browsing the website as guests, as well as information relating to user inquiries, service quality, and interactions with the Group.

4. Methods of Information Collection

Information may be collected through one or more of the following methods:

4.1. Information Provided Voluntarily by the User

Information provided by the user at the user's initiative, including through the completion of forms, contacting the Group, registering for services, or using such services.

Automatic collection through the website's systems, including the recording of user actions, attempts to perform actions, and the use of additional technological tools.

4.3. Collection Through Service Providers

Collection through the Group's suppliers and service providers, including providers of IT services, data storage, data processing, and mailing services.
