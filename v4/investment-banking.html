Investment Banking & Strategic Transactions

Global Financial Access for Complex Wealth Structures

At UHY CAPITAL, we provide tailored private banking and strategic advisory solutions for high-net-worth individuals, families, entrepreneurs, and international stakeholders. Our approach combines global banking access, capital structuring, financing solutions, and cross-border coordination within a discreet and integrated framework designed to support long-term growth, liquidity, and continuity.

Strategic financial solutions

From the transaction to the structure around it.

01

Capital Raising, IPOs & Corporate Equity Solutions

We advise companies, entrepreneurs, and shareholders on private and public capital raising, equity transactions, and IPO processes across local and international markets. Our services include strategic planning, transaction structuring, investor coordination, and engagement with leading financial institutions.

Our approach combines strategic financial insight with precise execution capabilities, supporting growth, liquidity, and long-term expansion in competitive global markets.

02

International Banking & Foreign Bank Account Structuring

Through relationships with leading financial institutions in Israel and abroad, we provide clients with access to international banking platforms and tailored private banking structures.

Our services include assistance with foreign bank account establishment, cross-border financial coordination, and banking solutions aligned with the client's global activities and wealth structure.

03

Financing, Credit & Mortgage Solutions

We provide customized financing and credit solutions for high-net-worth individuals, families, and corporations through access to local and international funding sources.

Services include private credit structures, asset-backed financing, liquidity solutions, and strategic financial planning tailored to the client's financial complexity and long-term objectives.

04

International Family Representation & Advisory

We represent and advise international wealth families in their activities across Israel and global markets, providing comprehensive coordination, institutional connectivity, and ongoing strategic support.

Our services are tailored to families operating across multiple jurisdictions who require high levels of discretion, sophisticated planning, and coordinated management of complex financial and family-related matters.

05

Trust Establishment & Administration

We provide advisory and ongoing management services for trust structures tailored to wealthy families, asset holders, and international clients as part of long-term wealth preservation and intergenerational continuity planning.

These structures are designed to support asset protection, privacy, wealth preservation, and efficient succession planning across generations and jurisdictions.
